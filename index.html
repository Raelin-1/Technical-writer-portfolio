Explainer

The difference between trading stocks and stock CFDs. Including formulas to calculate PnL for each type of transaction.

Stocks (or shares) represent a partial ownership of a company that allows the holder to claim its earnings and assets. Your return on investment depends on the success or failure of that company. If the company performs well and generates profits, its stock price is likely to increase. The more stocks you have, the greater your ownership stake and profits become.

Stock CFD (Contract for Difference) is a financial instrument. It allows speculation on the prices of financial assets, such as shares, Forex, and precious metals, without owning them. You and your broker enter a contract to exchange the difference between the opening and closing value of the financial product. CFD is a complex, high-risk method of investment because a small deposit provides exposure to a larger position that magnifies potential profits and losses.

Key differences between Trading Stocks and Stock CFDs

FeatureStocksStock CFDs
OwnershipYou own sharesYou do not own assets; you only speculate on the prices

DividendsYou receive dividends if holding shares at the ex-dividend dateYou may receive dividend adjustments (positive or negative) depending on long/short position
Trading CostsDepending on the country often include commissions, exchange fees, and stamp dutySpreads and/or commissions, no stamp duty in many jurisdictions
Market AccessMust trade during exchange hoursCan trade 24/5 or longer depending on broker

PnL (Profit and Loss) Calculations

1. PnL for Stocks

For stocks, profit or loss is calculated based on the difference between the buy and sell price, multiplied by the number of shares, minus commissions or fees.

Formula:

PnLstock = (Psell − Pbuy) × N − Fees

Where:

  • Psell = sell price per share;
  • Pbuy = buy price per share;
  • N = number of shares;
  • Fees = total commissions/fees.

Example:

Buy 100 shares at $50, and sell at $55, with a $10 total commission:

PnL = (55 − 50) × 100 − 10 = 500 − 10 = 490 USD in profit

2. PnL for Stock CFDs

With CFDs, PnL is based on price difference × number of contracts (or equivalent shares). Since CFDs are often leveraged, small price movements can result in larger gains or losses.

Formula:

PnLCFD = (Pclose − Popen) × N − Fees/Spread

Where:

  • Popen = entry price;
  • Pclose = exit price;
  • N = number of CFD units (usually 1 CFD = 1 share);
  • Fees/Spread = broker costs.

Example:

Open long 100 CFDs at $50, and close at $52, $5 total cost:

PnL = (52 − 50) × 100 − 5 = 200 − 5 = 195 USD in profit

If leverage was 1:5, you only needed $1000 to open a $5000 position.

Bonus game rules

Бонусная игра

Бонусная игра начнется, когда на барабанах выпадут три иконки часов.

На экране появится циферблат, показывающие системное время устройства.

На часах в случайном месте расположен скрытый будильник. Задача игрока — установить золотую стрелку на одно из делений циферблата, как можно ближе к отметке будильника, но не пересечь ее. Если во время движения стрелок будильник сработает, бонусная игра завершится. Если же часовая стрелка достигнет заданного игроком положения, то игрок получит выигрыш и вторую попытку, чтобы подобраться к отметке еще ближе.

Чем больше разница между текущим временем и выбранным, тем выше потенциальный приз.

Размер выигрыша варьируется от 0 до 2 400 ставок.

Bonus game

The bonus game starts when three clock symbols appear on the reels.

A clock displaying the current system time of the device will appear on the screen.

A hidden alarm clock is randomly placed somewhere on the clock face. The goal of the game is to position the golden hand on one of the clock's divisions as close as possible to the hidden alarm without crossing it.

If the alarm goes off while the hands are moving, the bonus game ends. However, if the hour hand reaches the set position, you will earn points and get another attempt to move closer to the alarm's position.

The greater the difference between the current time and the chosen position, the higher is potential reward.

The payout ranges from 0 to 2,400 bets.
